# The Chinese Idiom Knowledge Base

## Introduction (English)

The Chinese Idiom Knowledge Base (CIKB) is a large-scale language resource built by the Institute of Computational Linguistics at Peking University between 2004 and 2009. It grew out of the Grammatical Knowledge Base of Contemporary Chinese, which already contained about 6,790 idioms. Today CIKB collects more than 35,000 Chinese idioms and enriches every entry with far more semantic and pragmatic information. The resource is intended for both human readers and machines.

Chinese idioms, or *chengyu*, are fixed and highly metaphorical multi-word expressions whose meaning cannot be read off from their parts. They are not merely part of a language but part of a nation's history, society and culture, and their form has stayed remarkably stable for centuries. Most entries are four characters long and trace back to classical texts, yet they remain in active use in modern writing and speech. CIKB therefore records not only what an idiom means, but also how, when and why it is used.

The core collection presented here contains 3,457 high-frequency, high-value idioms, each described along fifteen dimensions. These cover pronunciation, variant forms, synonyms and antonyms, the Chinese definition, its classical source, example sentences, frequency in the *People's Daily* corpus, and English renderings such as literal and free translations together with close equivalents in English and other languages. Definitions and sources are almost entirely unique, while word frequency follows a long-tail distribution, from 0 to 33,066.

On this resource several natural language processing tasks have been explored. One experiment automatically classifies idioms as appreciative, derogatory or neutral with a support vector machine; combining character and word features reached an F1 score of 75.93%. Other directions include idiom token identification, event classification and teaching Chinese as a foreign language. The knowledge base thus serves both as a linguistic record of a national treasure and as a practical foundation for computational and educational applications.

## 成语知识库简介（中文）

汉语成语知识库（Chinese Idiom Knowledge Base，简称 CIKB）是北京大学计算语言学研究所于 2004 至 2009 年间构建的大规模语言资源。它脱胎于《现代汉语语法信息词典》，该词典当时已收录约 6,790 条成语。如今 CIKB 收录成语超过 35,000 条，并为每一条目补充了丰富得多的语义与语用信息。该资源既面向人类读者，也面向机器处理。

汉语成语是结构固定、高度隐喻化的多词表达，其整体含义无法从字面成分直接得出。成语不仅属于一种语言，更属于一个民族的历史、社会与文化，其形式数百年间保持高度稳定。多数成语为四字格，源自古代典籍，却仍在现代书面语与口语中被积极使用。因此，CIKB 记录的不仅是成语的含义，还包括它如何使用、何时使用以及为何使用。

本处呈现的核心成语集收录 3,457 条高频、高价值的成语，每条从十五个维度加以描述。这些维度涵盖读音、异形写法、近义与反义、中文释义、典籍出处、例句、《人民日报》语料词频，以及英文直译、意译和英语、其他语言中的对应表达等。释义与出处几乎逐条不同，而词频呈长尾分布，从 0 到 33,066 不等。

基于这一资源，已探索了多项自然语言处理任务。其中一项实验使用支持向量机将成语自动分为褒义、贬义和中性三类，结合汉字与词语特征的组合取得了 75.93% 的 F1 值。其他方向还包括成语实例识别、事件分类以及对外汉语教学。由此，该知识库既是民族瑰宝的语言记录，也是计算与教育应用的实用基础。
